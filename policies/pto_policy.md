# Arasaka Paid Time Off Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-201
**Version:** 1.3
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Policy Owner:** People Operations
**Applies To:** All full-time employees of Arasaka. Part-time employees receive prorated benefits as described in Section 7.

## 1. Purpose

Arasaka recognizes that time away from work is essential for employee health, well-being, and long-term performance. This policy explains how employees earn, request, and use paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and company holidays.

## 2. Vacation PTO Accrual

Full-time employees earn vacation PTO each pay period based on their length of continuous service:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO | Accrual per Semi-Monthly Pay Period |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 5.0 hours |
| 2 to 5 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 6.67 hours |
| More than 5 years | 25 days (200 hours) | 8.33 hours |

PTO begins accruing on the employee's first day of employment. New employees may use accrued PTO after completing **90 days** of employment.

## 3. Carryover and Maximum Balance

Employees may carry over up to **five (5) days (40 hours)** of unused vacation PTO into the next calendar year. Any unused PTO above this amount is forfeited on December 31, unless state or local law requires otherwise.

Employees may not accrue more than **1.5 times** their annual PTO allowance at any time. Once this cap is reached, accrual pauses until the balance drops below the cap.

## 4. Requesting PTO

- PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal.
- Requests of **three (3) or more consecutive days** must be submitted at least **two (2) weeks in advance**.
- Requests of fewer than three days should be submitted at least **two (2) business days in advance** when possible.
- Managers should approve or decline requests within five business days. Requests are generally approved on a first-come, first-served basis, taking business needs into account.
- PTO may be taken in increments of no less than **four (4) hours**.

During the company's annual fiscal close, the last two weeks of March, PTO requests from Finance and Accounting staff require approval from the department director.

## 5. Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from vacation PTO. Full-time employees receive **ten (10) days (80 hours)** of sick leave at the start of each calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out when employment ends.

Sick leave may be used for:

- The employee's own illness, injury, or medical appointments
- Caring for an ill family member
- Preventive care, including routine medical and dental visits

Employees must notify their manager **before the start of their scheduled workday** when taking sick leave. Absences of more than **three (3) consecutive working days** require a note from a healthcare provider.

Employees who are sick but well enough to work may be eligible to work remotely under the Remote Work Policy instead of using sick leave.

## 6. Company Holidays

Arasaka observes the following paid holidays each year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. In addition, each employee receives **two (2) floating holidays** per year, which may be used for any personal, cultural, or religious observance. Floating holidays do not carry over.

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed the following Monday.

## 7. Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees regularly scheduled for at least 20 hours per week earn vacation PTO and sick leave on a prorated basis according to their scheduled hours. For example, an employee scheduled for 30 hours per week earns 75% of the full-time allowance.

## 8. Other Leave

Arasaka also provides bereavement leave (up to five days for an immediate family member), jury duty leave, and parental leave (16 weeks of fully paid leave for birthing and non-birthing parents). These are described in separate policies.

## 9. Payout at Separation

When employment ends, accrued but unused vacation PTO is paid out at the employee's final base rate of pay. Sick leave and floating holidays are not paid out.

## 10. Questions

Questions about PTO balances or this policy should be directed to People Operations at the HR help desk.
