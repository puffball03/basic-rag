# Arasaka Remote Work Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-104
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025
**Policy Owner:** People Operations
**Applies To:** All full-time and part-time employees of Arasaka

## 1. Purpose

Arasaka is committed to giving employees the flexibility to do their best work while keeping the collaboration, mentorship, and culture that come from working together in person. This policy sets out how employees may split their working time between an Arasaka office and an approved remote location under a hybrid work model.

## 2. Hybrid Work Model

Effective March 1, 2025, Arasaka operates a hybrid work model. Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **three (3) days per week** and are expected to work from their assigned Arasaka office for a minimum of **two (2) days per week**.

- Each team designates at least one shared **anchor day** per week, when all team members are expected to be in the office. Anchor days are set by the team's manager and published on the team calendar.
- Employees choose their remaining in-office day in coordination with their manager.
- Remote days may not be "banked" or carried over. Unused remote days in one week do not add to the allowance for the following week.

## 3. Eligibility

Hybrid work is available to employees whose roles can be performed effectively outside the office. Eligibility is determined by the employee's manager in consultation with People Operations. The following roles are generally **not eligible** for remote days:

- Facilities, security, and reception staff
- Laboratory and hardware engineering roles requiring on-site equipment
- Employees in their first **30 days** of employment, who are expected to work fully on-site during onboarding

Managers may revoke hybrid eligibility for an employee who is on a documented performance improvement plan.

## 4. Approved Remote Locations

Remote work must be performed from the employee's primary residence, as recorded in the HR system, or another location approved in advance by the employee's manager. Employees must remain within their country of employment while working remotely. Working from another country for any length of time requires written approval from both People Operations and the Legal department because of tax and immigration requirements.

## 5. Availability and Working Hours

Employees working remotely are expected to:

- Be available during their team's **core hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.** local office time.
- Keep their status in the company messaging tool accurate.
- Respond to messages from colleagues within a reasonable time, generally within two hours during core hours.
- Attend scheduled meetings with their camera on unless there is a reasonable need to turn it off.

## 6. Equipment and Home Office Setup

Arasaka provides each hybrid employee with a company laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset. Employees who work remotely at least one day per week are eligible for a **one-time home office stipend of $500** to buy items such as a desk or chair. Stipend claims must be submitted through the expense system within 90 days of the employee's first remote day, following the Workplace Expense Policy.

Employees also receive an **internet reimbursement of up to $50 per month** while enrolled in the hybrid program.

## 7. Information Security

Remote work must comply with all Arasaka information security standards. In particular:

- Company devices must connect to Arasaka systems through the corporate VPN.
- Confidential documents may not be printed at home.
- Work must not be performed on public Wi-Fi networks without the VPN enabled.
- Screens must be locked whenever the device is left unattended.

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center immediately.

## 8. Requesting Changes

Employees may request an adjustment to their hybrid schedule, such as a temporary increase in remote days for a personal or family situation, by submitting a request to their manager through the HR portal. Temporary arrangements may be approved for up to **four (4) weeks** at a time. Requests for fully remote work as a long-term arrangement are handled as accommodation requests by People Operations.

## 9. Compliance

Failure to follow this policy, including repeated failure to attend anchor days, may result in the loss of hybrid eligibility and further disciplinary action. Managers are responsible for applying this policy consistently across their teams.

## 10. Questions

Questions about this policy should be directed to People Operations at the HR help desk.
