# Arasaka Remote Work Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-104
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026
**Policy Owner:** People Operations
**Applies To:** All full-time and part-time employees of Arasaka
**Supersedes:** HR-104 Version 2.0 (Hybrid Work Model)

## 1. Purpose

Arasaka believes that in-person collaboration is essential to innovation, mentorship, and the speed at which we deliver for our clients. This policy sets out Arasaka's full return-to-office requirement and the limited circumstances in which an employee may work remotely.

## 2. Return to Office

Effective September 1, 2026, all Arasaka employees are required to work from their assigned Arasaka office **five (5) days per week**. The hybrid work model introduced in Version 2.0 of this policy, including team anchor days and the allowance of up to three remote days per week, is discontinued.

Standard office hours are **9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.** local office time, with a one-hour lunch break. Managers may approve a shifted schedule (for example, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.) as long as the employee is on-site during core hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

## 3. Sick-Day Remote Work Exception

The **only** circumstance in which an employee may work remotely is when the employee is sick but well enough to work. This exception exists to keep contagious illness out of the office while allowing employees who feel able to work to do so from home.

To use a sick-day remote work day, the employee must:

1. Notify their manager through the HR portal or the company messaging tool **before 9:00 a.m.** on the day in question.
2. Mark the day as "Remote – Sick" in the HR system.
3. Remain available during core hours, as they would on an office day.

The following limits apply:

- Employees may use sick-day remote work for no more than **three (3) consecutive working days**. If the employee is still unwell after three days, they must either return to the office or take sick leave under the Paid Time Off Policy.
- Employees may use no more than **ten (10) sick-day remote work days per calendar year**.
- An employee who is too unwell to work should take sick leave rather than work remotely. Sick-day remote work is never required.

Sick-day remote work days do **not** count against an employee's sick leave balance. Remote work for any other reason, including caregiving, home repairs, travel, or personal preference, is not permitted under this policy and requires the use of paid time off.

## 4. Accommodations

Employees who need to work remotely because of a disability or medical condition lasting longer than the sick-day limits above may request a workplace accommodation through People Operations. Accommodation requests are reviewed individually and are not handled by managers directly.

## 5. Transition Provisions

- The one-time home office stipend and the monthly internet reimbursement offered under Version 2.0 are **discontinued** as of September 1, 2026. Claims for expenses incurred before that date must be submitted by **September 30, 2026**.
- Company equipment issued for home use, including monitors and headsets, may be kept at home for sick-day remote work or returned to the employee's office. Laptops must be brought to the office daily.
- Employees who relocated away from their assigned office while the hybrid model was in effect should contact People Operations to discuss their options before September 1, 2026.

## 6. Information Security

When working remotely under the sick-day exception, employees must follow all Arasaka information security standards, including connecting through the corporate VPN, never printing confidential documents at home, and locking their screen whenever the device is unattended. Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center immediately.

## 7. Attendance Tracking

Office attendance is recorded through badge entry. Managers will review attendance reports monthly. An employee who is absent from the office without approved paid time off, sick leave, or a properly logged sick-day remote work day will be considered to have an unexcused absence.

## 8. Compliance

Repeated unexcused absences or misuse of the sick-day remote work exception may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Managers are responsible for applying this policy consistently across their teams.

## 9. Questions

Questions about this policy should be directed to People Operations at the HR help desk.
