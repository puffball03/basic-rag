# Arasaka Workplace Expense Policy

**Policy Number:** FIN-310
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Policy Owner:** Finance Department
**Applies To:** All employees of Arasaka who incur business expenses on behalf of the company

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out which business expenses Arasaka will reimburse, the spending limits that apply, and how employees submit and get approval for expense claims. Its goal is to make sure company funds are spent responsibly while employees are not out of pocket for legitimate business costs.

## 2. General Principles

All reimbursable expenses must be:

- **Necessary** for conducting Arasaka business
- **Reasonable** in amount, reflecting what a prudent person would spend
- **Documented** with an itemized receipt
- **Submitted** within the required time frame

Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would their own. When in doubt about whether an expense is reimbursable, employees should ask their manager **before** making the purchase.

## 3. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel or make purchases regularly may be issued an Arasaka corporate card. Corporate cards must be used only for business expenses. Personal charges on a corporate card are prohibited, even if the employee intends to repay them. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in the expense system within **15 days** of the statement date.

## 4. Approval Thresholds

| Expense Amount | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $500.01 to $2,500 | Direct manager and department director |
| $2,500.01 to $10,000 | Department vice president |
| Over $10,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Purchases may not be split into smaller transactions to avoid a higher approval threshold.

## 5. Meals

- **Business travel meals:** Reimbursed up to a daily limit of **$75 per day** within the United States and **$100 per day** for international travel.
- **Client meals:** Reimbursed at a reasonable cost, generally up to **$150 per person** including tax and tip. The receipt must list attendees and the business purpose.
- **Team meals:** Team celebrations or working lunches require manager pre-approval and are limited to **$40 per person**.
- **Alcohol** is reimbursable only at client meals and may not exceed 30% of the total bill.

Tips are reimbursable up to **20%** of the pre-tax total.

## 6. Travel

All business travel must be booked through Arasaka's approved travel booking platform.

- **Air travel:** Economy class for flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or more. Business class requires vice president approval.
- **Lodging:** Standard rooms at hotels within the city rate limit published in the travel platform. Upgrades are not reimbursable.
- **Ground transportation:** Taxis, ride-sharing, and public transit are reimbursable. Rental cars must be mid-size or smaller unless traveling with three or more colleagues.
- **Personal vehicle use:** Reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate. Commuting between home and the employee's regular office is not reimbursable.

## 7. Office Supplies and Equipment

Standard office supplies are available in each Arasaka office and should not be purchased individually. Equipment purchases such as software licenses, specialized hardware, or books for professional use require manager approval before purchase and must follow the approval thresholds in Section 4. Equipment bought with company funds remains Arasaka property.

## 8. Professional Development

Arasaka reimburses up to **$2,000 per employee per calendar year** for job-related conferences, courses, and certifications. Requests must be approved by the employee's manager before registration. Employees who voluntarily leave Arasaka within **six months** of a reimbursed course or certification may be asked to repay the cost.

## 9. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable:

- Personal entertainment, including in-flight movies and hotel pay-per-view
- Traffic or parking fines
- Gym memberships and personal grooming
- Lost or stolen personal property
- Expenses for spouses, partners, or guests not involved in business
- Annual fees on personal credit cards

## 10. Submitting Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted through the expense system within **30 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Each item must include an itemized receipt for any expense over **$25**. Claims submitted more than **90 days** after the expense date will not be reimbursed. Approved reimbursements are paid with the next regular payroll cycle.

## 11. Compliance

Submitting false, inflated, or duplicate expense claims is a serious violation of this policy and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. The Finance Department audits expense reports regularly.

## 12. Questions

Questions about this policy should be directed to the Finance Department.
